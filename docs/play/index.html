        Baghchal rules

        Four tigers start on the corners. The goat side has 20 goats and moves first.

        1. Placement. Until all 20 goats are on the board, each goat turn places one goat on an empty point.
        2. Movement. Afterwards a goat steps along a line to an adjacent empty point.
        3. Tigers step along a line, or jump over one goat in a straight line to the empty point behind it, capturing that goat.
        4. Diagonal lines only pass through points whose coordinates sum to an even number.

        Tigers win by capturing 5 goats. Goats win when no tiger can move. The game is a draw on threefold repetition or after 100 plies without a placement or capture. If the goats have no legal move, the tigers win.

        Engines

        • Greedy: picks the best move by a one-move look-ahead.
        • Minimax: iterative-deepening alpha-beta search with a transposition table.
        • MCTS: Monte Carlo tree search with heuristic playouts. Live analysis uses it too.
        • TigerNet (legacy): the original convolutional net from this repository (tiger only).
        • Neural (loaded): any policy-value ONNX model that follows the contract in PLAN_RL_SELFPLAY.md, which is the target of the self-play training plan. Both sides are supported.
